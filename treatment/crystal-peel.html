Crystal Peel

Treatment Description

Skin Treatment with Crystal Layer. Crystal Peel is a powerful exfoliating treatment designed to address uneven skin tone, acne problems, scar problems, and hyperpigmentation. This advanced treatment uses crystal microdermabrasion to gently remove the outer layer of dead skin cells, revealing fresher, smoother skin underneath. Ideal for those looking to improve acne problems, reduce inflammatory pigmentation, reduce wrinkles, minimize pores, and lessen the appearance of fine wrinkles.

Benefits
  • Improves acne problem
  • Reduces inflammatory pigmentation
  • Reduce wrinkles
  • Reduce pores
  • Lessens the appearance of fine wrinkles
